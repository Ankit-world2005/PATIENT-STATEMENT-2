# App Brief

App name: My Patient

One-line idea: clinic can se their patient statement	

Problem it solves: particular client can check the patient statements and the clinics can also add their remarks in the logs and insurance company also.

Who it is for: this portal is used for clinics , once the company specialist update and upload the patient statement so the clinics can check only their patients.

The main action a user should complete: the specialist(user) upload the patient statement to the portal for the particular clinic , so the Clinic(user) can see the patient statement and logs. 

Must-have features:
- website should login page. 
- have 2-step authentication.
- specialist have have their dashboards and uploading/import patient statement options. 
- Website should be user friendly. 

Nice-to-have features:
- after login the first page have only three cards(AR Report , Clinic collection , Patient Statement) in middle of page and nothing extra on this page, where only patient statement can work and all the updated details are there , other cards are non-working.
- Company LOGO(US24 Solutions) are placed at top-most right corner. 
- USER(name) are placed at top-most left corner.
